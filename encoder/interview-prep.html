01

How to use this page

Interviews test two things: do you understand it, and can you explain it in under a minute. Practice both.

  • Flashcards (section 2): read the question and answer it out loud before flipping. Mark it Got it or Again. Your progress is remembered in this browser.
  • Quiz (section 3): 15 multiple-choice questions with explanations. Aim for 13/15 or better.
  • Drills (section 4): the whiteboard tasks interviewers like most. Do them on paper first, then check.
  • Question bank (section 5): every question in one searchable list, for quick revision.
A good spoken answer has three parts: what it is → why it exists → one concrete detail (a number, a shape, or a formula). For example: "We divide by √dₖ because dot products grow with dimension and saturate the softmax. With dₖ = 64, that's dividing by 8."
